My Journey

My career began in high-volume semiconductor manufacturing, where I led process improvement initiatives, analyzed production data, and collaborated with cross-functional teams to improve product quality and manufacturing performance. Today, I am a Firmware & Software Quality Lead in a robotics startup, leading a team responsible for firmware validation, release readiness, and quality process improvements. Throughout this journey, I discovered that combining engineering expertise with data analytics creates better decisions, more reliable products, and measurable business improvements. This portfolio showcases how I apply analytics, dashboards, automation, and structured problem-solving to solve real-world engineering and manufacturing challenges.
